01The office

Quiet Studio in Réunion

In Réunion, we work remotely with our clients on the island. The agency’s plans for growth run through the Indian Ocean.

For Auto Réunion Développement, a car dealership in Saint-Louis, the team created the visual identity, developed the brochure website and set up the CRM. It also launched Google Ads campaigns: in three months, nearly four times as many leads, without doubling the budget, and each lead costs 55% less. Réunion is also one of the territories covered by Green Digital, a digital signage service in Overseas France, for which the team created the landing page.
