01The office

Quiet Studio in Occitanie

The Occitanie office opened in 2022 and is located at 787 rue Marius Petipa in Montpellier. From there, the agency also supports projects across mainland France. In touch with the overseas diaspora in southern France, this office is your single point of contact for succeeding in Overseas France.

For Dormez Français, a premium bedding brand made in France and sold only online, Quiet Studio supported every step: brand image, visual identity, product design, photography, online store, advertising, results tracking and logistics. In less than three months, the brand went from a blank page to selling online.

Why an office in Montpellier when your clients are in Overseas France?

This office connects us with the overseas diaspora, well established in the south of mainland France, both for projects and for recruiting talent. It also brings us closer to some Paris decision-makers. And projects in mainland France with very high marketing standards sharpen our expertise, for the benefit of our clients in Overseas France.

Which sectors do you work in, in Occitanie?

Very different sectors: solar energy, construction, agriculture and online retail, with Dormez Français.

Do you take on young people from Overseas France as interns?

Yes, Quiet Studio takes on interns from Overseas France who are studying for a bachelor’s or a master’s degree. To apply, go to Join us.
