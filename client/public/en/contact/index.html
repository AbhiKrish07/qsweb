05FAQ

Got questions?

Answers to the questions we hear most often. For anything else, the team responds Monday to Friday.

Contact us
What kinds of projects do you work on?

From strategic consulting to web development, our work covers four areas: strategy and consulting, brand and creative, web and digital, campaigns and media. In practice, that ranges from creating a brand identity to rolling out an acquisition campaign, including e-commerce sites, institutional portals and custom business tools.

Does Quiet Studio work outside Overseas France?

Yes. Our office in Occitanie supports communication and marketing projects across mainland France, with the same range of services. Being in both places also lets us roll out programs across several territories when a client needs a consistent brand from French Guiana to Occitanie.

What does a typical engagement look like?

Every project starts with a kickoff meeting: your goals, your audiences, your timeline and budget constraints. If the situation calls for it, we audit what already exists before making any recommendation. Then comes the action plan, followed by production, with regular check-ins and results tracking.

Do you specialize in one industry?

No, and the overseas market doesn’t allow it. In territories this size, a hyper-specialized agency wouldn’t last. The demand is the opposite: mastering every industry present locally, from public health to retail, from culture to local government. That’s what pushes us to understand each business in depth.

What are your CSR commitments?

We work on four fronts: low-impact digital, with sites designed to use fewer resources; accessibility, so that what we produce can be used by everyone; local roots, favoring local suppliers and talent; and responsible communication, which rules out promises our clients can’t keep.

What sets you apart from other agencies?

A project manager who lives in the territory where you operate. They know its customs, its language and its culture, which no brief can replace. Add a team that covers strategy, creative and technology in-house, and you get programs that actually work on the ground, not campaigns imposed from outside.
