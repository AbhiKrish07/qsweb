0304Method

One method, five steps

A brand, a campaign or a website: the path is the same, and you always know which step we’re at.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Scoping

    We listen to your goals, challenge them and put them in writing.

    A kickoff meeting: your goals, your audiences, your timeline and budget constraints. If the situation calls for it, an audit of what already exists before any recommendation.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Strategy

    A costed, prioritized recommendation you can decide on.

    Our people work together to design the strategy and the action plan.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Creative

    In-house production, with clear approval points.

    Website, branding, communication campaigns: this is where your project comes to life.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Rollout

    Launch, distribution, partner coordination.

    Regular check-ins until launch.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Measurement & review

    Results analyzed, adjustments proposed.

    Even after delivery, we look back. At the end of the engagement, we walk you through your project review.

What services does Quiet Studio offer?

Quiet Studio brings together four complementary services:  strategy & consulting,  brand & creative,  web & digital, and  campaigns & media. This setup lets us build consistent programs, from strategic thinking to producing materials and rolling out digital tools.

How do I choose the right service for my project?

It all depends on what you need to begin with. Clarifying your positioning or improving an existing program falls under strategy & consulting; creating a brand identity, under brand & creative; building a website, under web & digital; launching a campaign, under campaigns & media. We start by understanding your goals, your audiences and your constraints, then point you to the right combination of services.

How does a communications consulting engagement work?

A consulting engagement starts with an analysis of your context, your existing communication and your goals. We can then work on your positioning, your communication strategy, your media plan or your brand platform, with a prioritized action plan suited to your resources.

Are Quiet Studio’s services suited to projects in Overseas France?

Yes: it’s our home ground. Quiet Studio was founded in French Guiana in 2012 and has five offices: French Guiana, Martinique, Guadeloupe, Réunion and Occitanie. Our project managers come from the territories where they work, close to you. Practices, cultures, media, constraints: they know them from the inside. Our consulting, creative, web and campaign work starts from that knowledge, for programs that are right in substance and in execution.
