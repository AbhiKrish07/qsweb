02Our commitments

Every action counts

To have a positive impact on the environment, society and our team, every action counts. And to contribute, there’s nothing better than putting our expertise to work for causes that matter.

  1. 01

    Reduce and offset
    our emissions

    We publish our GHG report every year and reduce our emissions, from emails to travel. We are also committed to offsetting our footprint by supporting sustainable, local initiatives.

  2. 02

    Supporting
    short supply chains

    We favor local hiring and work with suppliers close to our offices. This proximity guarantees the quality of our services while strengthening human ties within our communities.

  3. 03

    Well-being
    at work

    Flexible hours, a pleasant work environment, collaborative workshops and team-building activities. Remote work is also encouraged to support work-life balance.

  4. 04

    Respecting
    personal data

    Beyond standards such as the GDPR, we collect and use data transparently and ethically, only to the extent necessary for our digital campaigns. Our clients’ trust comes first.

What are your CSR commitments?

We work on four fronts: low-impact digital, with sites designed to use fewer resources; accessibility, so that what we produce can be used by everyone; local roots, favoring local suppliers and talent; and responsible communication, which rules out promises our clients can’t keep.

What labels and certifications has Quiet Studio earned?

In May 2023, Quiet Studio became the first agency in Overseas France to earn the “Engagé RSE” (Committed to CSR) label from AFNOR Certification, at the Confirmed level; the label was renewed in 2025. The agency is also a Meta Partner, Google Partner and Dust Solution Partner, an AI solutions provider for SMEs and mid-sized companies, a France Num Activator, a member of FEDOM and a partner of ACCDOM.
