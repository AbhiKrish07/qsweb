Mobapi

Website for Mobapi, an industrial monitoring platform born at Quiet Studio: a multilingual site, in French and English, designed for operations, maintenance and energy teams.

The project

How do you show that a single platform brings together machines of every brand and every generation?

Client
Mobapi
Industry
Retail & manufacturing
Services
Brand & creative, Web & digital
Location
French Guiana
Visit the website of Mobapi (opens in a new tab) View the visuals

01The challenge

Mobapi is a vendor-independent industrial monitoring platform. It brings equipment of every brand and generation together on a single screen, including older equipment and equipment at remote sites, along with the readings teams take on their rounds.

Born at Quiet Studio in Cayenne out of a research and development project, Mobapi has become an independent startup. It began as an API platform, then the product narrowed its focus to industrial monitoring, as close to the machines as possible. The new website had to say so from its very first line.

The challenge: convince operators, maintenance teams and energy managers, whose first question is whether it works with their machines, without losing the old site’s URLs or its existing search rankings.

02The solution

Quiet Studio designed a predominantly light website, whereas none of the competing sites we studied opens on a light background. Each section opens with the reader’s question, and the site structure follows job roles: Operations, Maintenance, Energy, then the Mixed fleets, Rounds and shift log, Platform and Compatibility pages, where visitors look up their equipment or protocol.

The project includes:

  • a study of competing websites;
  • site structure and copy, in French and English;
  • Mobapi’s brand guidelines applied: midnight blue, greens and the Galano typefacethe brand guidelines: midnight blue, greens and the Galano typeface ;
  • custom isometric illustrations, drawn and then animated;
  • field photos, all treated with the same midnight blue overlay from the brand guidelinesfield photos, all under the same midnight blue overlay ;
  • screens from the Mobapi.EYE app;
  • news in French and English, including in-depth articles written for SEOnews in French and English, including in-depth articles ;
  • a demo request form;
  • cookie-free audience measurement, so no banner to click;
  • development and launch;
  • a motion design explainer video, in landscape and portrait formatsa motion design film, in landscape and portrait formats.

03The results

The website is live at mobapi.com.

  • the site opens in the language of the visitor’s browser, French or Englishthe site opens in the browser’s language ;
  • the old site’s URLs redirect to the new pages: existing search rankings are preservedexisting search rankings are preserved ;
  • pages are prepared for search engines as well as AI assistantspages prepared for search engines and AI assistants alike.
