Kazad

Marketing campaign automation and competitive intelligence software

A clean product database, flyers prepared for your studio, your competitors’ catalogs collected for you. Kazad keeps a record of your offers; your team keeps the final say.

Request a demo

01The problem

Catalogs keep coming, spreadsheets can’t keep up

A file of EAN codes with abbreviated product names, photos to track down, a flyer to prepare in a few days, then to proofread line by line. Meanwhile, competitors’ catalogs pile up. From one promotion to the next, nothing is kept.

  • Born in our studio

    Quiet Studio prepares flyers and catalogs for its clients. Kazad grew out of that work, so that what’s already known never has to be typed in again.

  • Your team approves

    Kazad suggests a match or flags a discrepancy in a laid-out flyer. A person confirms before the data goes into the database.

  • Data that builds up

    Every import, every promotion and every catalog collected adds to the price history.

02The modules

Your products, your campaigns, your competitors

Three modules that work together or on their own. For each one: what works today and what’s coming soon.

Product data

A clean database that keeps a record of your offers. On import, Kazad recognizes the products it already knows, flags possible duplicates and files each record with its images.

Today

  • Import by EAN code or product name, from a spreadsheet or past flyers
  • Custom fields, variants and bundles, several databases
  • A database that feeds your campaigns
  • Regular and promo price history, by promotion

Coming soon

  • Pre-filled records for new products
  • The same database for your websites
  • On each product record, the prices of equivalent products at other retailers in your area

Marketing campaigns

From product selection to a feed for your studio. Kazad prepares the EasyCatalog feed, then checks the proof of the finished flyer against your selection.

Today

  • Product selection, promo prices and callouts for each promotion
  • EasyCatalog feed for flyers and catalogs
  • Proof check: prices, missing or extra products, product names; your team decides

Coming soon

  • Automatic social media posts
  • Google Ads campaigns
  • Shelf talkers and enriched labels

03Use case

A retailer prepares its next flyer

From the purchasing office’s file to a checked flyer: what Kazad does today, in five steps.

  1. 01

    Import

    The purchasing office’s file arrives, with its EAN codes, abbreviated names and prices. Kazad recognizes the products it already knows and reuses their records; the team decides on possible duplicates.

  2. 02

    Complete

    Known products keep their records and images; the team completes the new ones. Pre-filling of their title, description and photo is coming soon.

  3. 03

    Compare

    Before setting promo prices, the team looks at the latest catalogs collected from retailers in its area and compares them with its own database.

  4. 04

    Prepare

    The selection, with its promo prices and callouts, goes to the studio as an EasyCatalog feed: the designer lays out the flyer without typing the products in again.

  5. 05

    Check

    The laid-out flyer is checked against the original list: prices, missing or extra products, product names. The team approves, and the promotion’s prices join the history for the next one.
