Club
Beauté

Structuring the visual identity of Parfumerie Club Beauté with a new logo, a brand book and communication materials tailored to the world of perfume and beauty.

The project

How do you give a perfume and beauty retailer an identity as desirable as its products?

Client
Parfumerie Club Beauté
Industry
Retail & manufacturing
Services
Brand & creative
Location
French Guiana
View the visuals

01The challenge

Parfumerie Club Beauté needed a more consistent identity to assert its positioning in a highly visual, competitive world.

The brand image needed to evoke expertise, beauty and pleasure, while staying flexible enough to cover the retailer’s various needs.

The challenge was to create a graphic system that could work across a variety of media:

  • social media;
  • brand profile;
  • editorial materials;
  • magazine;
  • business cards;
  • sales documents;
  • print and digital materials.

02The solution

Quiet Studio structured Parfumerie Club Beauté’s identity around several deliverables:

  • logotype creation and adaptations;
  • design of the brand book;
  • definition of graphic rules;
  • creation of a social media profile;
  • design of templates for social media posts;
  • creation of templates for magazine materials;
  • business card designs;
  • harmonization of print and digital materials.

The brand book serves as a shared reference to keep the retailer consistent, whatever the medium.

The art direction strikes a balance between the premium world of perfume, the closeness of personal advice and the visual impact social media requires.

03The results

Parfumerie Club Beauté now has a clearer brand foundation and a consistent set of materials to build out its communications.

The project makes it possible to:

  • increase recognition of the retailer;
  • harmonize communication materials;
  • professionalize the brand’s presence on social media;
  • make it easier to create new content;
  • adapt the identity to print and digital materials;
  • asserting Parfumerie Club Beauté’s positioning in the world of beauty and cosmeticsassert Parfumerie Club Beauté’s positioning ;
  • give the team a graphic framework they can use right away.
