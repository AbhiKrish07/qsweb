How do you launch a 100% digital brand, from product design to e-commerce, in just 3 months? That’s the challenge we took on with Dormez Français, a new DNVB (Digital Native Vertical Brand) of high-end bedding, which Quiet Studio supported across the entire value chain: branding, identity, product design, photo shoots, Shopify site, acquisition, tracking and logistics.

A real entrepreneurial adventure, carried out hand in hand with the client, and a demonstration of our ability to create a DNVB bedding brand from a blank page. Here’s how we turned an idea into a brand that draws interest… and sells.

Key takeaways

  • Goal: create from scratch a design-led, sustainable, made-in-France bedding brand, sold exclusively online.
  • A 360° strategy built on 5 pillars: branding, product design, Shopify e-commerce, acquisition and tracking, data and logistics.
  • Client-side and server-side tracking that captures 30 to 40% more signals.
  • Less than 3 months from blank page to launch.

The challenge: build everything, take everything digital

Dormez Français didn’t exist. The ambition was to create and grow a design-led, sustainable bedding brand sold exclusively online (a pure player), starting from a blank page.

The challenge: invent everything

Name, identity, visual world, product design, choice of materials, e-commerce experience, marketing and data tools: everything had to be invented, with a high-end, made-in-France positioning.

Why Quiet Studio?

Dormez Français chose Quiet Studio for our creative AND technical expertise, our ability to deliver fast and our command of a DNVB’s e-commerce challenges.

Our methodology: a 360° strategy built on 5 pillars

To meet this challenge, Quiet Studio rolled out a comprehensive strategy built around 5 complementary pillars.

1. Branding

  • Creation of the name, the logo and the entire visual identity
  • Design of the brand guidelines and typefaces
  • Art direction on photo and video shoots
  • Rollout of the brand world across all channels

To go further on this topic, find out how to build a strong visual identity.

2. Product design and art direction

  • Co-design of the products: choice of materials, colors, textures
  • Work on the sensory experience and the promise of comfort
  • Support on photo and video shoots, indoors and out in nature

Example

Dormez Français’s photo and video shoots alternated between indoor scenes and natural settings, all the way up to the heights above Lac du Salagou!

3. Shopify e-commerce

  • Creation of a custom Shopify site (UI/UX, development, custom components and modules)
  • Integration of branding and product content
  • Implementation of custom components (from Figma to Shopify)
  • Reassurance features: 100-night trial, fast delivery, made in France, secure payment, etc.

Still undecided about the platform? Read our comparison WordPress or Shopify: which CMS should you choose for your website.

4. Acquisition and tracking

  • Orchestration of creative marketing campaigns and partnerships (co-branding)
  • Implementation of client-side and server-side tracking (Google Tag Manager, Meta, TikTok Ads, Google Ads…) for precise monitoring and detailed analysis of marketing performance
  • 30 to 40% more signals measured, better attribution and optimized ROI

5. Data and logistics

  • Automated processing and export of data (orders, logistics, eco-tax, package and weight calculations)
  • Real-time transmission to the logistics provider, with custom CSV data exports, to ensure service quality and optimize costs
  • Fast delivery and reliable tracking, even for bulky products like bedding

Collecting and using data properly is a job in itself: we explain how to collect data by the rules.

Frequently asked questions

What is a DNVB?

A DNVB (Digital Native Vertical Brand) is a brand born online that designs its own products and sells them online. Dormez Français is a pure player: its products are sold exclusively online.

How long did it take to launch Dormez Français?

Less than 3 months from blank page to launch, covering branding, product design, the Shopify site, acquisition, tracking and logistics.

Why set up server-side tracking?

Combined with client-side tracking, it captured 30 to 40% more signals, improved sales attribution and optimized campaign ROI.
