Why is a communications audit essential?

The specific challenges of Overseas France

Businesses in French Guiana, Martinique, Guadeloupe and Réunion face unique challenges:

  • Digital competition from mainland France: big national brands often dominate search results.
  • Cultural nuances: your communication needs to resonate with the local identity.
  • Tight budgets: an ever-larger share of marketing budgets goes to digital, but resources remain limited.
  • Infrastructure still catching up: about 71% of premises in Martinique could be connected to fiber in September 2026 (ANCT), compared with nearly 95% nationwide (Arcep, March 2026).

A fast-changing digital landscape

Artificial intelligence is rewriting the rules: ChatGPT claims more than 900 million users every week (OpenAI, February 2026). And locally, WhatsApp is king: 99% of internet users in Martinique used it, according to the 2024 Awitec study. Your customers now expect instant, personalized, location-based answers.

What is a 360° communications audit?

Definition and scope

A 360° communications audit is a comprehensive analysis of your digital ecosystem that examines:

  • your positioning against local competitors and those from mainland France;
  • your digital channels: website, social media, SEO, ads;
  • your audience: who follows you, who ignores you, and why;
  • your content: effectiveness, consistency, local relevance;
  • your conversions: from visitor to customer, where are you losing prospects?

Quiet Studio’s 5 angles of analysis

  1. Technical audit: site performance, SEO, security, mobile first.
  2. Competitive audit: positioning against local players and those from mainland France (to get a first idea, see how to analyze your competitors’ online ads).
  3. Content audit: relevance, cultural fit, engagement.
  4. Social media audit: presence, interactions, community management.
  5. Conversion audit: customer journey, friction points, optimization.
