02Method

One method, five steps

A brand, a campaign or a website: the path is the same, and you always know which step we’re at.

One method, five steps

  1. 01

    Step 1: Scoping

    We learn who you are before we pick up a pencil.

    Where you come from, what you stand for, who you are speaking to. If you already have a logo or brand guidelines, we study them before making any proposal.

    We listen to your goals, challenge them and put them in writing.

    A kickoff meeting: your goals, your audiences, your timeline and budget constraints. If the situation calls for it, an audit of what already exists before any recommendation.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Strategy

    We define the brand’s visual direction.

    It translates your positioning into visual choices. You approve it before creative work begins.

    A costed, prioritized recommendation you can decide on.

    Our people work together to design the strategy and the action plan.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Creative

    Drawing, photo, video, layout: everything is produced in-house.

    Before finalizing, we send you a proof so you can check that everything is right.

    In-house production, with clear approval points.

    Website, branding, communication campaigns: this is where your project comes to life.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Rollout

    The guidelines are applied, one medium after another.

    You receive the files, ready to print or publish.

    Launch, distribution, partner coordination.

    Regular check-ins until launch.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Measurement & review

    An identity isn’t measured in clicks.

    You leave with the brand guidelines and the files: your teams and your vendors work from the same rules.

    Results analyzed, adjustments proposed.

    Even after delivery, we look back. At the end of the engagement, we walk you through your project review.

Where should you start when creating your visual identity?

With your fundamentals: your story, what you do, your audience, your goals and your values. Everything then starts from the company name, followed by the logo.

What makes a visual identity strong?

It makes you identifiable (people understand what you do), remarkable (people notice you) and memorable (people remember your name and talk about it). And above all, it inspires trust.

What is a brand book for?

It sets the rules for your visual identity: logo, colors, typefaces, images. It serves as a reference for your teams and your partners alike. It is the document to share with your vendors before ordering any design work or a redesign of your website.

What is the difference between a brand book and brand guidelines?

The two terms are often used interchangeably. At Quiet Studio, the brand book sets out the essential rules. The brand guidelines are a more complete document: they include many mockups across different media, delivered as editable files.
