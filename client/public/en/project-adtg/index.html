ADTG

Brand book and website for the Agence de Développement Territorial de Guyane, an organization set up at the initiative of French Guiana’s territorial government to support project leaders, local governments and local initiatives.

The project

How do you make entrepreneurship support programs easier to understand in French Guiana?

Client
Agence de Développement Territorial de Guyane
Industry
Health & social services
Services
Strategy & consulting, Brand & creative, Web & digital
Location
French Guiana
Visit the website of ADTG (opens in a new tab) View the visuals

01The challenge

The Agence de Développement Territorial de Guyane (ADTG) was founded in 2023 at the initiative of French Guiana’s territorial government. Based in Saint-Laurent-du-Maroni, it supports project leaders and local governments across the territory, from west to east.

Its audiences have different needs: entrepreneurs, people outside the usual support systems, local governments, partners. The challenge was to present that diversity without losing the visitor.

Objectives:

  • explain the agency’s role clearly;
  • distinguish its programs and point everyone to the right one;
  • give ADTG an identity that is both serious and approachable.

02The solution

We started with the brand book: ADTG’s visual identity and visual world. Green, yellow, simple shapes, portraits, and short messages such as “From ideas to lasting projects.”

We then designed the website: site structure and content, mockups, then a site that adapts to phones. It organizes information into simple sections:

  • the agency, its departments and its key figures;
  • the Kréa’Pulse Guyane incubator and the “Impulsion” pre-incubation program;
  • social innovation and the fight against discrimination;
  • testimonials and an FAQ;
  • a form to contact the team.

03The results

The adtguyane.fr website is live. ADTG now has a brand identity and a website that present its role and programs simply. The incubator and the pre-incubation program each have their own page.

Visitors will find:

  • the incubator journey, from informal to formal;
  • the “Impulsion” pre-incubation program, for those with the furthest to go;
  • the agency’s key figures;
  • a “Let’s talk about your project” button that leads to the form.

ADTG manages its own content, editing and publishing it in-house.
